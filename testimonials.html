★ ★ ★ ★ ★

In From Fibromyalgia to Freedom: How I Cured My Chronic Condition, L.C. Hawkins opens up in a very candid way about the journey she embarked on to heal from an 'incurable' disease.

From the massive effort she exerted on a daily basis to accomplish simple tasks, to the mental blocks that tried to throw her off the road to recovery even when she thought she had found her way through the fog, Hawkins offers a glimpse of light, and the possibility that there is hope to end the suffering once and for all.

Her guide to healing offers a digestible roadmap that others may follow at their own pace. She is honest and realistic, without being pushy, and doesn't make pie in the sky promises. Hawkins reveals that the mindset shift doesn't happen instantly or overnight. But rather that it is a journey, and the road can be messy and frustrating, but that it's important to keep trying, again and again.

Highly recommended reading not only for those suffering from chronic pain, but to anyone feeling stuck and overwhelmed by the challenges of life. Hawkins' inspirational message of determination, patience, self love, and faith in a better future for yourself is well timed!
